Privacy Policy

Chomper ("we", "our", "us") provided by evensure AI Agency, is a web application that allows users to upload food photos to estimate calories and track daily intake. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our app.

⸻

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use Chomper:

Personal Information

• Name and basic profile information (entered during onboarding)

• Optional calorie target or nutrition preferences

Uploaded Content

• Food photos you upload to Chomper

• Metadata associated with the photos (file name, upload timestamp)

Usage Data

• Interaction with features (upload history, daily calorie tracking)

• Device type, browser type, and general analytics

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

⸻

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

• Identify and analyze food photos

• Estimate calorie count and display results

• Show your recent meal history

• Personalize your experience (e.g., using your name)

• Track daily calorie intake based on your goal

• Improve the app experience and functionality

Your data is used only for app functionality and not for advertising.

⸻

3. How We Store and Protect Data

• Uploaded photos and user data are stored securely on our servers or trusted cloud providers.

• We employ encryption and industry-standard security practices to protect data.

• Access is restricted to authorized personnel only.

We make reasonable efforts to protect your data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

⸻

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share data only with:

• Cloud storage or AI model providers required to analyze the food photo

• Service providers that help us operate the application (all under confidentiality agreements)

We may disclose data if required by law, such as responding to legal requests or protecting our rights.

⸻

5. Photo Handling & Retention

• Food photos are used solely for calorie analysis.

• If you delete your account (feature coming soon), all associated data will be removed.

⸻

6. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

• Access your data

• Request deletion of your data

• Edit your personal information

• Withdraw consent for data processing

To make a request, contact us at: evensureagency@gmail.com

⸻

7. Children's Privacy

Chomper is not directed to children under 13 years old.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

⸻

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a new date.

⸻

9. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:

Email: evensureagency@gmail.com

Website: evensure.agency
